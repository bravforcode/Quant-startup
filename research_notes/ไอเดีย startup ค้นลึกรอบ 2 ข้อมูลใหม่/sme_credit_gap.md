# Thai SME credit access gap: primary-source notes (as of 20 Oct 2026)

Scope: Bank of Thailand (BOT) publications, OECD, NCB, GSB, OSMEP and MOF primary material where retrievable; press used only where labelled.

**Labels**
- **FACT**: stated in a primary publication (BOT, OECD, NCB, GSB, OSMEP, MOF, or a BOT statistics table). URL and date given.
- **ESTIMATE**: my arithmetic on FACT inputs. Method given.
- **OPINION**: a stated view by a company or official, not a measured figure.
- **UNVERIFIED**: press report only, including press quotes of speeches for which the BOT transcript was not retrieved.

## 1. Origin of the 60-70%, 78% and 86% figures

**Finding:** all of these come from one speech by BOT Governor Vitai Ratanakorn at the TNN Future Forum 2026 on 9 Sep 2026. No BOT primary text with these numbers was retrieved. The BOT's own news page for that date (https://www.bot.or.th/th/news-and-media/activities/activities-20260909.html, 9 Sep 2026) is FACT for the three measures and the 70,000 / 200,000 mn baht figures, but the fetched text did not contain the rejection sentences.

| Figure | What the governor said (as reported) | Source (URL, date) | Label |
|---|---|---|---|
| 60-70% | SME rejection "มาโดยตลอด" (consistently) about 60-70%. The Standard frames it as the actual rate including SMEs rejected before entering the formal system. | Prachachat, https://www.prachachat.net/finance/news-2064274, 9 Sep 2026 (direct quote). The Standard, https://thestandard.co/thai-sme-crisis-central-bank/, 9 Sep 2026 | UNVERIFIED (press quoting speech) |
| 78% | New SMEs with no financial history. Prachachat's access figure of 22% implies the same 78%. | Prachachat (above); Nation, https://www.nationthailand.com/business/banking-finance/40070829, 9 Sep 2026 | UNVERIFIED |
| 86% | SMEs with an existing bad-debt record. Prachachat gives 14% access. | Prachachat (above) | UNVERIFIED |
| 90% | "อาจสูงถึง 90%" (possibly up to 90% in practice); governor said 78% and 86% are underestimates. | The Standard (above); Bangkok Biz, https://www.bangkokbiznews.com/finance/1251208, 9 Sep 2026 | UNVERIFIED |
| 55% (in-system) | Prachachat quotes the governor: in **2025**, loan applications from **existing SME borrowers in vulnerable groups** were 45% approved and 55% not approved. The Standard generalises this to all SME applications. | Prachachat (above); The Standard (above) | UNVERIFIED. Scope in the press is narrower than the headline "55%" implies. |
| ">50% of requests not granted" | TNN: most requests were from existing customers. | TNN, https://www.tnnthailand.com/wealth/investment/246403/, 9 Sep 2026 | UNVERIFIED |

**Official status of rejection data:** the OECD Thailand country page says the rejection-rate series, SME interest-rate series and percentage of SME loan applications are "no longer provided due to a change in data collection methodology" (OECD, https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/financing-smes-and-entrepreneurs-2026_bb7fc5c4-en/thailand_82ede51e-en.html, 22 Sep 2026). **FACT:** no regular published BOT rejection-rate series was found. The governor's figures are speech-level estimates.

## 8. Conflicts found in the sources

1. **The "55%" in-system rejection** is scoped to 2025 existing SME borrowers in vulnerable groups (Prachachat). The Standard presents it as the rate for SMEs in general. Use the narrower scope.
2. **Large-firm access for top-30% SMEs:** 61% in Nation and Bangkok Biz; 63% in Prachachat, TNN, Daily News and PIER (via Nation, 8 Oct 2026).
3. **Large-firm interest rate:** about 3% vs 3.9% in the same M&B article.
4. **SME growth, Q2 2026:** -4.6% (MGR, Krungsri, M&B) vs -5.07% (Nation, 5 Sep 2026). The BOT PDF does not state either.
5. **Quarters of contraction:** 15 (Nation, 5 Sep 2026) vs 16 (BOT via MGR, 18 Aug 2026). The 16 figure is consistent with BOT's 13 quarters in Dec 2025.
6. **Credit Boost volume:** over 70,000 mn (BOT, 9 Sep 2026) vs 80,000 mn (press, 8 Oct 2026).
7. **Credit Boost start:** 15 Jan 2026 (BOT press release) vs 23 Feb 2026 (TNN speech report).
8. **SME share of employment:** 70% (governor) vs 68.8% (OSMEP 2024, FACT) and 68.81% (OECD, FACT). The governor's 13.6 mn is consistent with OSMEP's 2025 figure of 13,601,129 (FACT, OSMEP dashboard).
9. **SME "growth" 1%** (governor): this is SME contribution to GDP growth, not SME GDP growth. OSMEP reports SME GDP growth of 3.1% in 2024 (FACT, OSMEP SME report 2025) and 0.84% YoY in Q2/2569 (FACT, OSMEP dashboard). Different measures.
10. **Guarantee target:** 100,000 mn a year for the guarantee alone (BOT, Oct 2026); 200,000 mn a year combined with BSB (BOT, Sep 2026); "100,000 mn in 1-2 years" (BOT, Dec 2025). Different time bases.
11. **NCB coverage:** 92% (M&B, 18 May 2026) vs 60% (NCB PR, 29 Jun 2026). Use NCB's primary release.
12. **SME NPL:** 9.16% (Sep 2026) vs about 9.5% (Oct 2026). Both press.
13. **Series break in BOT SME loan data:** FI_CB_022 shows about 3.05 trn baht (domestic banks, Q2/2568). FI_CB_021 shows about 2.07 trn baht (all banks, Jul 2026). The foreign-branch portion is only about 20 bn baht, so the gap is methodology (OSMEP definition with credit-limit adjustment), not scope. Do not compare levels across these two tables.
