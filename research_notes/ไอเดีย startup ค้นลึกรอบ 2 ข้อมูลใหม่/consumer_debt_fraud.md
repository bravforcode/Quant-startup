## 1. Household debt and non-performing loans

### 1.1 Household debt to GDP
- Q2 2026 (provisional): household loans THB 16,432,156 mn; household loans to GDP 85.2%. Q1 2026 (revised): 86.0%. Q4 2025: 86.8%. [FACT] Bank of Thailand table EC_MB_039_S3, https://app.bot.or.th/BTWS_STAT/statistics/BOTWEBSTAT.aspx?language=ENG&reportID=1004 (undated; Q2 2026 provisional).
- Same 85.2% for Jun 2026; series range 40.6% (Mar 2003 low) to 95.5% (Mar 2021 high). [FACT] CEIC compilation of BOT data, https://www.ceicdata.com/en/indicator/thailand/household-debt--of-nominal-gdp (undated; data to Jun 2026).
- End-2025: household debt THB 16.44 trn = 86.7% of GDP (NESDC, citing NCB). [FACT] Nation Thailand, https://www.nationthailand.com/business/economy/40066636 (pub. 2026-05-25).
- Q1 2026 first published at 85.9%, "lowest in six years"; outstanding THB 16.41 trn, +0.5% YoY; SCB EIC reads this as deleveraging under tight credit, not stronger household finances. [FACT for data, OPINION for interpretation] SCB EIC flash, https://www.scbeic.com/en/detail/file/product/10147/hknkoi227d/Flash-Household-debt-JUL-EN-20260723.pdf (pub. 2026-07-23).
- Q2 2026: BOT says ratio did not rise despite slower GDP growth because debt balances fell and banks kept selling distressed debt; ratio "among the highest in Asia" (Reuters). [FACT as reported] https://www.bangkokpost.com/business/general/3303779/thai-bank-lending-picks-up-bad-loans-edge-down (pub. 2026-08-18). The article gives end-March debt as 85.9% of GDP, which conflicts with the 86.0% revised figure above (UNVERIFIED which vintage is current; the BOT table is the reference).
- KResearch (KBank): Q2 2026 85.2%; balance ~THB 16.4 trn, +0.6% YoY; forecast of ~84.0% by end-2026. The 84.0% is a forecast. [ESTIMATE] https://www.ts-asset-mgmt.com/report/th/2026-10-12-thai-household-debt-ratio-eases-q2-as-debt-balance-turns-up (pub. 2026-10-14).

### 1.2 Commercial-bank NPLs
- Q2 2026: Stage 3 (NPL) THB 534.8 bn; NPL ratio 2.82% (Q1 2026: THB 535.8 bn, 2.85%). Stage 2 ratio 6.78%. Loan growth +2.0% YoY (corporate-led; SME and consumer loans contracting). [FACT] Bank of Thailand, https://www.bot.or.th/en/news-and-media/news/news-20260818.html (pub. 2026-08-18); Q1 brief https://www.bot.or.th/en/news-and-media/news/news-20260519.html (pub. 2026-05-19).
- Q2 2026 NPL inflows about THB 110 bn: new defaults THB 52 bn; loans returning to NPL after restructuring about THB 52 bn. [FACT] as reported citing BOT/NCB, Nation Thailand, https://www.nationthailand.com/business/banking-finance/40070729 (pub. 2026-09-07). Re-defaults after restructuring are roughly half of inflows.
- BOT governor: overall NPL level "not unusually high", but SME deterioration warrants concern. [FACT as reported] same source.

### 1.3 Consumer NPLs (NCB data, >90 days overdue)
- Consumer NPLs THB 1.31 trn = 9.59% of total loans (prior quarter 9.45%), driven by housing and personal loans. Special-mention (31–90 days) THB 480 bn = 3.51% (prior quarter 3.09%). [FACT] NCB data as cited by NESDC, Nation Thailand https://www.nationthailand.com/business/economy/40066636 (pub. 2026-05-25). The quarter the NPL figure refers to is not stated in the article (UNVERIFIED period).
- Stage 2 + 3 share of consumer loans 10.7% in Q1 2026 (from 11.1%); hire-purchase (HP) Stage 2 + 3 share 15.0%, about 2x other consumer categories. [FACT] BOT data as cited by SCB EIC (https://www.scbeic.com/en/detail/file/product/10147/hknkoi227d/Flash-Household-debt-JUL-EN-20260723.pdf, pub. 2026-07-23).
- NCB CEO (Sep 2026): more SM loans turning into NPLs; nano-finance has a higher share of >90-day loans than other types; consumption personal loans and HP for phones, computers and household goods show higher NPL levels. [FACT for data, OPINION for interpretation] Nation, https://www.nationthailand.com/business/banking-finance/40070729 (pub. 2026-09-07).

### 1.4 Loan growth and borrower segments
- Q1 2026: commercial-bank household loans -2.1% YoY (8th straight quarterly decline). Credit card and personal loan companies -0.1% YoY (6th straight decline). Car and motorcycle HP -7.5% YoY. [FACT] BOT data as cited by SCB EIC (pub. 2026-07-23).
- Under-25s: credit-card debt +13.5% and personal-loan debt +11.5% in 2025, the fastest growth of any age group. [FACT] NESDC, Nation Thailand (pub. 2026-05-25).
- Ages 20–35 ("first jobbers"): debt ratio 52.7%; NPL share 27%; ~25.5 mn Thais in debt. Cited by BOT Governor Vitai Ratanakorn (Jun 2026). [FACT as attributed; underlying survey not checked] Nation, https://www.nationthailand.com/business/banking-finance/40067039 (pub. 2026-06-04).
- NCB: Pico Finance operators (provincial retail lenders supervised by the Fiscal Policy Office) are not NCB members, so they cannot see existing debts before lending. Some e-commerce platforms are applying for NCB membership. [FACT as attributed to NCB CEO] ThaiPBS World, https://www.thaipbsworld.com/economy/gen-z%E2%80%99s-penchant-for-debt-worrying-for-the-thai-economy (pub. 2026-06-27).
- NCB coverage (~80% of household debt): Q2 2026 household debt of THB 13.67 trn on NCB's basis (not the full BOT total; scope difference). [FACT as attributed] Thairath, https://en.thairath.co.th/money/economics/thai_economics/2956845 (pub. 2026-09-02).

## 2. Debt collection, restructuring, and existing solutions

### 2.1 Regulation
- Debt Collection Act B.E. 2558 (2015): contact allowed Mon–Fri 08:00–20:00 and public holidays 08:00–18:00 (s.9); no threats, violence, profanity, or disclosure of debt to third parties (s.8, s.11); no false impersonation of courts, lawyers or officials (s.12); unfair fee or cheque inducement banned (s.13). [FACT] DOPA English translation, http://report.dopa.go.th/laws/document/2/231.pdf (undated).
- Frequency cap: no more than one contact per day, per Debt Collection Supervisory Committee notification announced 30 Jul 2562 (2019), published in Royal Gazette 23 Aug 2562. [FACT] DOPA, http://report.dopa.go.th/laws/document/sub/2/207.pdf (announced 2019-07-30).
- Penalties: administrative fine up to THB 100,000 (s.34); criminal penalties up to 1 year or THB 100,000 (s.39); up to 5 years or THB 500,000 for threats or violence (s.41). [FACT] DOPA, http://report.dopa.go.th/laws/document/2/231.pdf.
- Top complaint offences 2017–2024 (DOPA): (1) threats or violence; (2) more than one contact per day; (3) disclosing debt to third parties; (4) contacting non-debtors; (5) contact at unnotified locations. No complaint counts appear in the snippet. [FACT] The Bangkok Insight, https://www.thebangkokinsight.com/news/politics-general/general/1381835/ (pub. 2024-09-11).
- BOT Responsible Lending Regulations: in force since 1 Jan 2024; eight areas include persistent-debt assistance, difficulty assistance, and legal action. Lenders must offer affordable restructuring before legal action, asset seizure, or debt transfer. Amendments (Notification of 31 Jan 2025) took effect 1 Jul 2025 for some criteria. [FACT] BOT, https://www.bot.or.th/content/dam/bot/fipcs/documents/FPG/2568/EngPDF/25680030.pdf (pub. 2025-01-31).
- Debt buyers and AMCs must set affordability-based repayment terms and notify borrowers of transfer. [FACT] same BOT document, section 8.2.
- 2026 debt-collection rule changes: none found in this pass. [UNVERIFIED] The Debt Collection Act and the 2019 frequency notice were the latest found.

### 2.2 Government restructuring programmes
- "You Fight, We Help" (คุณสู้ เราช่วย): registration closed 30 Sep 2025. Eligible registrants 940,000, covering ~THB 620 bn debt. About 620,000 (66%) had restructuring done by 30 Sep 2025, covering THB 440 bn (71%). About 80% of participating accounts returned to regular repayment. [FACT] BOT news 18 Nov 2025, https://www.bot.or.th/th/news-and-media/news/news-20251118-2.html.
- Earlier uptake: NCB data, as of 30 Apr 2025, showed only 16.5% of the target had signed contracts. [FACT] Money & Banking, https://en.moneyandbanking.co.th/2025/174481/ (pub. 2025-05-23).
- Original targets: ~2.1 mn accounts, ~1.9 mn debtors, ~THB 890 bn. Press reports ~820,000 applicants (about half the target) before the extension. [FACT as reported] Thai Times, https://thaitimes.com/thai-government-plans-debt-relief-for-millions-amid-growing-economic-pressures (pub. 2026-02-12).
- "Pay Off Debt Quickly, Move Forward" (ปิดหนี้ไว ไปต่อได้; English branding "Clear Debt, Move Forward"): launched 11 Nov 2025; registration opened 5 Jan 2026. Eligibility: individuals; NPL more than 90 days at 30 Sep 2025; total NPL across NCB-reporting lenders at most THB 100,000 per borrower. Phase 1 is for commercial-bank accounts: ~1.6 mn accounts, 1.2 mn borrowers, ~THB 43,600 mn debt. Total programme ~1.9 mn accounts (incl. ~330,000 SFI accounts via Ari-AMC). Funded by a reduction in the FIDF fee of 0.23% in 2025. Roughly 4.7 mn accounts were NPLs eligible under the THB 100,000 cap (60% of NPL borrowers). [FACT] BOT speech, https://www.bot.or.th/content/dam/bot/documents/th/news-and-media/speech/speechgov-11nov2025.pdf (pub. 2025-11-11); BOT news, https://www.bot.or.th/th/news-and-media/news/news-20251111.html (pub. 2025-11-11).
- Options: (1) pay-to-close, with a principal reduction; (2) instalments over up to 3 years, interest-free during the programme, with principal reduction. Terms reported in 2026 differ by source: Thai Times (Feb 2026) reported a 10% pay-to-close proposal; SAM+ and Money & Banking (Aug–Sep 2026) reported 50% reduction (paid by 31 Dec 2569) and 30% reduction for instalments through 30 Jun 2572. [UNVERIFIED which terms are final; check bot.or.th/cleardebt] https://kaoupdate.com/2026/08/18/100748-sam/ (pub. 2026-08-18); https://en.moneyandbanking.co.th/2026/218576/ (pub. 2026-01-08).
- Uptake: ~90,000 registrations in the first 3 days (SAM). [FACT] Money & Banking, https://en.moneyandbanking.co.th/2026/218576/ (pub. 2026-01-08). More than 100,000 applicants in the first week. [FACT as reported] Bangkok Post, https://www.bangkokpost.com/business/general/3174283/bad-debt-scheme-sees-100000-sign-up (pub. 2026-01-12).
- Conflict: SAM says the programme covers 1.2 mn borrowers with "combined debt over THB 4.36 bn". The BOT speech says ~THB 43,600 mn. [UNVERIFIED; the BOT figure is likely correct, the SAM figure may be a decimal error]
- "Debt Restructuring Agency": no standalone agency found. The operating entities are SAM (state "social AMC", for bank NPLs) and Ari-AMC (for SFIs). [UNVERIFIED that no such agency exists; not found in search] A government plan was reported citing Prime Minister's Office sources: a THB 10 bn debt haircut for ~3 mn debtors whose debts total ~THB 120 bn, targeting long-standing low-value debts and farmers (BAAC borrowers over 70). [UNVERIFIED status] Thai Times, https://thaitimes.com/thai-government-plans-debt-relief-for-millions-amid-growing-economic-pressures (pub. 2026-02-12).
- SAM debt clinic ("คลินิกแก้หนี้ by SAM"): for unsecured NPLs over 120 days; interest 3–5% p.a.; up to 10 years; total debt up to THB 2 mn. [FACT as company announcement] Money & Banking, https://moneyandbanking.co.th/2026/267583/ (pub. 2026-09-03).
- SAM+ app (iOS and Android): ID, e-KYC, debt balance, plan selection, contract signing, and QR payment for the restructuring programme. [FACT as company announcement] Kaoupdate, https://kaoupdate.com/2026/08/18/100748-sam/ (pub. 2026-08-18).
- BOT Q2 2026 brief: commercial banks continue pre-emptive restructuring, which contains new NPL formation. [FACT] https://www.bot.or.th/en/news-and-media/news/news-20260818.html.

## 3. Scam and fraud losses

### 3.1 Official figures
- AOC 1441 (Digital Economy ministry; press release covers 1 Nov 2023 to 14 Nov 2024; the statistics table covers 1 Nov 2023 to 31 Oct 2024): 1,176,512 calls to hotline 1441; 348,006 suspicious accounts suspended; damages ~THB 19 bn. Facebook 26,804 cases (THB 718 m); call centres 22,299 cases (THB 945 m); websites 16,510 cases (THB 1,148 m). [FACT] Public Relations Department, https://www.prd.go.th/th/content/category/detail/id/39/iid/340072 (pub. 2024-11-15).
- BOT annual report 2024: 23,717 scam victims in Dec 2024 (26,575 same period 2023). Money-siphoning app victims fell from ~3,000 (Jan 2024) to 1 (Dec 2024). Up to 1,750,000 mule accounts suspended in 2024. [FACT] https://www.bot.or.th/en/research-and-publications/reports/annual-report/report-2024/box07.html (undated; report 2024).
- Cross-bank Central Fraud Registry (CFR) data sharing live from 1 Aug 2024; banks had acted on 15,000+ names reported to the CFR since Q4 2023 (TBA chairman). [FACT] BOT, https://www.bot.or.th/en/news-and-media/news/news-20240902.html (pub. 2024-09-02).
- BOT measures: no SMS with links; facial scan for transactions of THB 50,000 or more; one mobile-banking account per bank; 24-hour fraud hotline per bank. [FACT] BOT annual report 2024 (as above).
- 2025 full year: >363,000 online fraud cases and ~THB 24.57 bn losses; ETDA handled 39,112 complaints. Source attribution in the article is unclear. [UNVERIFIED] Tech Collective, https://techcollectivesea.com/2026/09/03/thailand-scam-epidemic-technology-problem/ (pub. 2026-09-03); also SendTech Times, https://stechtimes.com/en/article/thailand-fraud-losses-put-realtime-scam-controls-to-the-test-mtppieqi (pub. 2026-09-06).
- Conflict: the same 2025 year is reported by Chiang Rai Times as 405,929 cases and THB 23.40 bn for ages 20–49 alone. That is larger than the 363,000 total above. [UNVERIFIED; definitions differ] https://www.chiangraitimes.com/hot-news/crime/ai-deepfake-scams-thailand/ (pub. 2026-02-03).

- Jan–Jul 2026: THB 9.3 bn in losses. [UNVERIFIED official source] Thai Times, https://thaitimes.com/thailand-reports-9-3-billion-baht-in-online-scam-losses-as-government-tests-ai-defences (pub. 2026-10-20).

  - 9–15 Aug 2026: 6,060 complaints; THB 260 m; Bangkok 138 cases, THB 63.3 m. [FACT] Nation Thailand, https://www.nationthailand.com/news/general/40069932 (pub. 2026-08-18).

- Police Technology Crime Suppression Division (TCSD) (22 Aug 2026): 397,223 mule accounts identified 1 Oct 2025 to 15 Aug 2026; damages THB 40.54 bn; mule-account count for 1–15 Aug 2026 down 60.3%, damages down 86.9%; 1,222 ACSC cases 1 Oct 2025 to 20 Aug 2026 (70.62% resolved); 145,582 scam URLs blocked. [FACT as stated by police; the baseline for the "down" percentages is not given] MGR Online, https://en.mgronline.com/politics/tcsd-reveals-mule-accounts-down-603-pushes-hard-on-gambling-scammers-and-online/ (pub. 2026-08-22).

### 3.3 Enforcement and policy
- Mule-account data sharing: a draft notice would list "mule accounts of every colour". Banks would share data in real time through the CFR, AMLO, and AOC 1441 data, and suspend a person's accounts at every bank. [FACT for the plan; OPINION for whether it works] DES ministry, reported in Nation Thailand (pub. 2026-08-09).
- Refund rules: a ministerial regulation was planned for 12 Aug 2026, letting victims apply to AMLO for refunds without a court order. Completion was targeted for Sep 2026. [UNVERIFIED that it took effect] Nation (pub. 2026-08-09).
- TCC has begun legal action against online platforms and financial institutions over consumer responsibility. [FACT as reported] Nation (pub. 2026-08-09).
- Scam-URL blocking: 145,582 URLs blocked with platforms (police, pub. 2026-08-22, above).

### 3.4 Bank fraud-management pain points (vendor survey)
- BioCatch survey of Thai banks (Mar 2026): 87% say their bank struggles to identify mule networks across institutions; 78% of C-suite say fraud losses are rising; 39% rate their controls 5/10 or below; 24% fully investigate a case within one day; 73% rank reputational risk above financial impact. [OPINION; vendor survey, sample size not stated] https://www.biocatch.com/press-release/thai-banking-leaders-admit-they-struggle-to-identify-interbank-laundering-networks (pub. 2026-03-11).

## 4. BNPL: scale, regulation, and competitors

### 4.1 Scale
- BNPL accounts ~620,000 (2021) to 4.91 mn (2024); outstanding BNPL loans THB 17.9 bn in 2024 (+38% YoY); data from 6–8 major providers. [FACT] BOT data as reported by Thai Enquirer (pub. 2026-06-08) and Nation (pub. 2026-06-04). Earlier BOT survey figure: 600,000 accounts and THB 6.83 bn in 2021 to ~5 mn accounts and THB 17.9 bn in 2024 (Nation, pub. 2026-06-04).
- BOT estimates 5–6 mn users in 2025; industry estimates up to 7 mn. [ESTIMATE] Thai Enquirer (pub. 2026-06-08). BOT Governor said ~6 mn accounts in 2025, nearly 10x 2021 (Business Times, pub. 2026-06-23).
- Shopee: more than 1 mn users (per Business Times; Shopee declined to comment). [OPINION] https://www.businesstimes.com.sg/international/asean/buy-now-pay-later-106-baht-bubble-tea-highlights-thailands-household-debt-risk (pub. 2026-06-23).
- Market-size estimates conflict across vendors: Research and Markets reports 2025 at USD 3.94 bn (Feb 2025 release) and, in a Q1 2026 databook, 2025 at USD 528.7 mn. [UNVERIFIED; vendor estimates that do not reconcile] https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250221411775/en/ and https://www.researchandmarkets.com/reports/5305003/thailand-buy-now-pay-later-business-and (pub. 2025-02-21 and Q1 2026 update).
- Usage patterns: "eat now, pay later" installments on 50-baht chicken rice over 4 months and 106-baht bubble tea over 3 months (BOT Governor; Nation, pub. 2026-06-04). Default opt-in at checkout (BOT: "some platforms set BNPL as default"). Interest often 16–18% (BOT, as reported by Thai Enquirer); longer plans up to 25% p.a. (Business Times).

### 4.2 Regulation
- Current framework: digital personal loan licence (BOT-regulated) capped at THB 20,000 and 25% interest; or Civil and Commercial Code lending with no limit and 15% interest cap (BOT governor, Nation Jun 2026). [FACT as stated by BOT] https://www.nationthailand.com/business/banking-finance/40067039.
- Proposed BOT and MOF rules: licence required from the minister (MOF announcement under Announcement No. 58); BOT draft notification. Public hearing 16 Oct to 15 Nov 2026, two rounds of 30 days. [FACT] BOT public hearing page, https://www.bot.or.th/th/laws-and-rules/public-hearing/public-hearing-20261016-BNPL.html (pub. 2026-10-16); Thai PBS, https://www.thaipbs.or.th/news/content/571262 (pub. 2026-10-19); Bangkok Post, https://www.bangkokpost.com/business/general/3340118/central-bank-seeks-feedback-on-buy-now-pay-later-rules (pub. 2026-10-19).
- Target: BNPL licence in Q4 2026, with a transition period for existing operators; ~6 major providers in a preliminary survey. [FACT as stated by BOT; timeline could move] Nation, https://www.nationthailand.com/business/banking-finance/40070654 (pub. 2026-09-04).
- Guidelines under consideration: minimum borrower age; product-category screening; minimum purchase value; opt-in credit (no default enrolment); affordability checks for larger purchases; interest caps. Supervision of "credit for purchases on online platforms" regardless of rate, including civil-code lenders. [FACT as stated by BOT, not yet final] Nation (pub. 2026-06-04) and Thai Enquirer (pub. 2026-06-08).
- BOT says some digital-platform BNPL providers are not under supervision and do not report their service data to BOT, so household-debt monitoring is incomplete. [FACT] BOT public hearing page (pub. 2026-10-16).

### 4.3 Competitors (named, with what is public)
- Atome: 3-instalment, interest-free BNPL; welcomed the framework. [OPINION, company] Business Times (pub. 2026-06-23).
- K PAY LATER (Kasikornbank): instalments 1, 3, or 5 months; limit THB 20,000; via K PLUS. [FACT, product terms] Fintech News Singapore, https://fintechnews.sg/top-3-bnpls-in-thailand-and-whats-next-for-the-market/ (pub. 2026-01-07).
- SPayLater (Shopee): run by Cmoney Capital since 4 Aug 2023. Cmoney Capital reported 2023 revenue THB 1,429.52 mn and profit THB 6.59 mn (group-level, not BNPL-only). [FACT] DBD figures as reported by Marketeer, https://marketeeronline.co/archives/381875 (pub. 2024-10-10).
