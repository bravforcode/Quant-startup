# Global scan (non-Southeast Asia): late payment, home/elder care, permitting, SME finance

Research date: 2026-10-20. Scope: US, UK, EU, Japan, Korea, plus global startups. Southeast Asia excluded (e.g., Jia, Philippines, was found and excluded).

## Label key

- **FACT**: figure published by an official statistical or government body (or a regulator/central bank), or a count taken directly from a named primary dataset. Survey-based official releases are labelled FACT with the method noted.
- **ESTIMATE**: modelled, projected, extrapolated, or survey-based figure from a non-official or commissioned source. Also used for official *projections* (future demand).
- **OPINION**: advocacy, testimony, company claims, or forecasts without data behind them.
- **UNVERIFIED**: date, method, or number could not be confirmed, or the figure comes only from an aggregator or secondary blog.

Notes on comparability: sources define "late payment", "vacancy", "rejection" and "permit time" differently. Figures within one section are not directly comparable across sources. No cross-source ranking of problem sizes is made here, and no business ideas are recommended.
