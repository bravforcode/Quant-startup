# Thailand e-Tax Invoice & e-Receipt: status as of 2026-10-20

Scope: Revenue Department (RD, กรมสรรพากร) rules on e-Tax Invoice & e-Receipt (ใบกำกับภาษีอิเล็กทรอนิกส์ / ใบรับอิเล็กทรอนิกส์), SME routes, incentives, accredited Service Providers, pricing, and source conflicts.

Method: Exa web search (highlight excerpts) plus one web fetch of the RD "new law" page. Most documents were read from search excerpts, not full text. Items marked UNVERIFIED need the full document before use.

Labels: FACT (official) = RD, ETDA, Cabinet/government, or Royal Gazette material. ESTIMATE = official forecast or arithmetic on sourced numbers. OPINION = commentary. UNVERIFIED = secondary, vendor, undated, or not confirmed in an official text.

Date convention: BE (พ.ศ.) converted to CE. 2569 BE = 2026 CE. 2570 BE = 2027 CE. Where a source shows no date, that is stated.

---

## 1. Bottom line

- **No legislated B2B mandate found.** RD and ETDA state the e-Tax system is currently not enforced. The ministerial regulation says electronic invoicing "may" be used (อาจ). FACT.
- **The only dated official "end" is 31 Dec 2570 (= 31 Dec 2027), and it is an incentive, not a mandate.** Royal Decree No. 808 (B.E. 2569) was listed by RD on 14 Oct 2569 (2026-10-14). FACT.
- **The "2028" target is UNVERIFIED.** It appears only in a commercial blog (NAWA, 2026-08-13) that cites unnamed "trade publications". No RD, ETDA, or Cabinet source states a 2028 deadline.
- **SME route:** e-Tax Invoice by Time Stamp (ETDA calls it "e-Tax Invoice by Email"). Free, for VAT-registered businesses with revenue of 30 million baht or less. FACT.
