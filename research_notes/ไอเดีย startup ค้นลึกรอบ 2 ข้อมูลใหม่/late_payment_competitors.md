# Competitor map: invoice collection, receivables management, invoice financing, factoring and supplier-payment tracking for SMEs in Thailand and Southeast Asia (status as of 2026-10-20)

## Method and labels

- Retrieval date for every row: 2026-10-20. Discovery used Exa web_search_exa (highlight text from search results). I did not complete full-page reads for every row, so details come from search highlights; check any row before relying on it.
- Most pages show no publication date. Where none is shown, the row says "undated"; the retrieval date applies.
- Labels: FACT = stated in a source I read (company self-descriptions are marked "self-reported"). ESTIMATE = my arithmetic on sourced numbers. OPINION = a third party's characterisation. UNVERIFIED = could not confirm, single secondary source, or conflicting sources.
- "NOT FOUND" = no public information located in this pass. Nothing has been filled in by inference.
- Scope: 61 in-scope entities (Section A to F below). Funding Societies (including its Indonesian brand Modalku) and Investree are each counted once. Adjacent or defunct entities are listed separately and are not counted.
