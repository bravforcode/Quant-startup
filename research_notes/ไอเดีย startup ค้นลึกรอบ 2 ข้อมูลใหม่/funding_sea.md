Research date: 2026-10-20. Sources were retrieved with the Exa search tool. The Exa server showed an ERR_PROXY_TUNNEL connection notice at session start, but the search calls returned results. The Tech in Asia 2025 report URL returned no readable content and is not used.

**Labels:** FACT = from a funding database or company/press announcement (secondary citations are marked). ESTIMATE = my calculation or inference. OPINION = interpretation. UNVERIFIED = single self-reported, aggregator or blog source that I could not cross-check, or a date/amount I could not confirm. UNVERIFIED items are excluded from totals.

**Scope rules:** A company is counted as Thai if it is Thailand-headquartered or its main operations are in Thailand. Regional companies are counted only if they operate in Thailand. Companies with rounds outside 2025 to 2026 are listed for context and marked "out of window". Monk (US) was excluded. Stecon's investments in ALICE (US) and Asperitas (NL) were excluded because they are not Thai startups.

### 1a. Methodology differences

- **DealStreetAsia and Kickstart:** equity deals only, excluding debt, with corporate equity included. Their H1 2026 figure is about $7.25B. Their full-year 2025 figure is $5.37B.
- **Tracxn "SEA Tech":** tech companies only, using its own sector taxonomy. Full-year 2025 is $5.2B, and H1 2026 is $7.4B. Tracxn's Jan to Jul figure (as cited by Second Talent) is $12.8B, which conflicts with its H1 report. Tracxn is not internally consistent across its own reports.
- **Tracxn Thailand pages:** counts only companies Tracxn classifies as "funded", using the country of the company. The 2025 comparator for Thailand is much lower than the Dealroom and DealStreetAsia numbers.
- **Dealroom:** counts VC-backed companies by HQ and round size. The Thai widget only shows $15M+ rounds.
- **Domicile vs operations (ESTIMATE):** Singapore takes 91 to 94% of SEA tech funding in Tracxn's reports, which mostly reflects holding-company domicile. Several large rounds are Singapore-domiciled holding companies with operations elsewhere (e.g. DayOne). Thai figures are therefore understated when the operating country is Thailand but the HQ is Singapore.
- **Announced vs closed:** DayOne's Series C was announced at over $2B in January 2026 and later reported at $4.5B. DealStreetAsia uses $4.5B. Tracxn uses the combined $4.5B across two tranches.
- **Sector classification:** Amity Solutions is classed as SaaS by Tracxn and Marketing by Dealroom.
