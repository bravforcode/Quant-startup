# Thai-language complaints and discussion, 2025-2026: research notes

Prepared: 2026-10-20. Window: items dated 2025-01-01 to 2026-10-20 unless marked OUT OF WINDOW.
Sources: Thai forum (Pantip), Thai news, government and agency releases, consumer-council (สภาองค์กรของผู้บริโภค / TCC) and NGO statements, vendor blogs (labelled).
Not covered: Reddit (blocked earlier); Google Play Thai-app reviews (no Thai app reviews were returned; one Filipino app listing was excluded as out of scope).

## Method and limits

- Searches: about 15 Exa web searches plus one 11-URL fetch batch, in three rounds.
- Pantip is a search index, not a full archive. Counts below are what the search returned, not totals.
- Pantip metadata often has no date. Dates are labelled:
  - FACT: a date printed in the post text or in search metadata.
  - ESTIMATE: interpolated from thread ID. Anchors: #43292538 = 2025-03-03 (metadata); #44036594 text states 31 Mar 2569 (2026-03-31). Rate about 57,000 IDs/month. Accuracy about +/-2 months.
- Frequency scale (applies to what this search surfaced, not true prevalence):
  - few = 1-3 distinct in-window items
  - some = 4-9
  - many = 10 or more, or several official/news reports with figures
- News and government items are over-represented compared with forum posts, so frequency is not comparable across topics.
- Several items are one event reported by several outlets. These are counted as events where noted.
- Labels: FACT (what the source states), ESTIMATE, OPINION (vendor, commercial, or advocacy source), UNVERIFIED.
- No personal identifiers, phone numbers, or individual names of posters are recorded.
